# Licensing status

This repository contains a proposed evaluation-and-commercial licensing model. The draft root `LICENSE` is not effective. It proposes free internal noncommercial development and testing, and a separate written ICxA commercial license associated with an eligible Corporate Membership for commercial use, production deployment, or public distribution. Membership alone is not a license.

The normative specification is separately reserved under `specification/LICENSE`. Trademark, endorsement and certification rights are not granted. Final terms are subject to ICxA rights verification and approval. Until effective terms are published, existing restrictions remain in force.
