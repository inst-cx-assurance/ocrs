# Code of Conduct

This repository is governed by a respectful, professional, and evidence-based collaboration model.

## Core expectations

- Treat all participants respectfully.
- Keep discussion focused on the specification, governance, evidence, and interoperability model.
- Separate technical disagreement from personal disagreement.
- Base proposals on the governing ICxA standards and the explicit OCRS model.
- Flag unresolved questions and decisions requiring ICxA approval.

## Professional standards

Participants should:

- maintain factual accuracy;
- avoid unsupported legal or compliance claims;
- distinguish between evidence, professional judgment, and governance authority; and
- preserve the separation between OCRS and the standards that govern authorization.

## Reporting issues

Any misconduct, abuse, or persistent disruption should be raised to the project steward or governance body responsible for the repository. The concern should be documented factual and specific, and handled according to the prevailing repository governance process.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all discussions, reviews, and repository contributions related to OCRS.
