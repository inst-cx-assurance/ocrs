# Conformance

## OCRS Data Conformant

Every declared schema/profile is supported; all package records pass Draft 2020-12 structural validation and OCRS semantic integrity rules, including evidence usability/scope and cross-record gate, review, recommendation, authority, decision, handover, and TOC consistency.

## OCRS Exchange Conformant

An implementation imports and exports exact identifiers, versions, relationships, provenance, inventory, qualifications, extensions, and controlled terms without semantic loss.

## OCRS RFDC Evaluation Conformant

An implementation accepts Data Conformant RFDC packages and returns the normative results and full explanation basis for the conformance scenarios reproducibly, without creating human decision records.

## OCRS ICxA Profile Conformant

Data and behavior conform to an approved, exact versioned ICxA mapping. The draft v0.1 mapping is marked for ICxA verification, so this claim is not currently established by the repository.

Data Conformance proves internal structural and semantic consistency for the declared versioned snapshot only. It does not prove that source records are truthful, that evidence is professionally sufficient, that field conditions remain unchanged, or that a system is ready. Deterministic evaluation is not professional judgment. Readiness Recommendations and Operational Readiness decisions do not constitute Outcome Authorization. OCRS conformance does not establish underlying ICxA Standards conformity or certification. OAP and ICxA certification policy remain outside v0.1.
