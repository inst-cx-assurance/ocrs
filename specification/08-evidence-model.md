# Evidence Model

## 1. Purpose

The evidence model represents the controlled records that demonstrate whether requirements or readiness conditions have been satisfied.

## 2. Evidence characteristics

The model shall support evidence records that include:

- provenance;
- traceability to the relevant requirement, readiness condition, or outcome evidence need;
- evidence type and method;
- identification of responsible parties;
- time and place of generation;
- document or digital object references;
- handling or chain-of-custody metadata where relevant; and
- the disposition of the evidence, including accepted, rejected, deferred, or superseded.

## 3. Evidence evaluation

Evidence is assessed separately from its generation. OCRS must preserve the distinction between:

- creating evidence through activity/test execution; and
- evaluating that evidence for sufficiency and adequacy.

The evidence evaluation function is not identical to technical validation and is not a substitute for Outcome Authorization.

## 4. Linkage to Level 1 and Level 2

Evidence generated in a Level 2 activity may support a Level 2 readiness recommendation and, where applicable, may also support an Outcome Evidence Need under an ICxA Level 1 assurance process. The model must preserve both relationships without collapsing them into one status.
