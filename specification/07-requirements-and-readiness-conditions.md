# Requirements and Readiness Conditions

This chapter distinguishes between outcome requirements and readiness conditions.

## 1. Requirements

Requirements describe what must be met in the context of a system, asset, subsystem, project, or boundary. They may arise from design obligations, project conditions, code or configuration requirements, functional performance conditions, or governance obligations.

## 2. Readiness conditions

Readiness conditions describe the criteria that must be satisfied before a stage-gate, transition, or readiness decision is supported. They may be derived from a commissioning plan, test conditions, activity completion criteria, or a defined readiness review gate.

## 3. Required separation

OCRS must preserve the distinction between:

- a requirement that defines what is to be satisfied; and
- a readiness condition that defines whether a transition or stage-gate is supported.

A requirement may contribute to a readiness condition, but the readiness condition is not automatically equivalent to a legal, regulatory, or outcome requirement unless the governing ICxA standard expressly says so.

## 4. Traceability

Each requirement and readiness condition should be traceable to:

- the relevant system, boundary, or asset;
- the evidence supporting satisfaction or non-satisfaction;
- the applicable activity or verification process;
- the formal review or recommendation decision; and
- the governing standard or profile context.
