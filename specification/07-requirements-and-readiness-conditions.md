# Requirements and readiness conditions

Execution Requirements state what Level 2 work or acceptance criteria apply. Readiness Conditions state how a declared gate criterion is evaluated for subjects and boundaries. They MUST remain separate.

An RFDC condition selects one deterministic rule: accepted evidence, prerequisite gate, boundary mapping, dependencies satisfied, no blocking constraints, or Deficiency controls. Gate membership and scope are checked across records. Evidence references requirements and conditions explicitly and must cover the condition's required subjects, boundaries, requirements, type, currency, and evaluation-time validity. The source reference and acceptance criteria remain visible, and uncertain ICxA mappings are marked `icxa_verification_required`.

Manual source statuses do not replace evaluation. The applicable profile, exact profile version, subject, boundary, and supporting record IDs form part of the result basis.
