# Level 1 Authorization Model

## 1. Governing authority

The Level 1 authorization model defines the governance process by which outcomes are authorized, residual risk is accepted, and outcome decisions are recorded under the governing ICxA standard.

## 2. Required components

The model supports:

- Outcome Obligation
- Outcome Requirement
- Outcome Evidence Need
- evidence that satisfies the evidence need
- independent evidence evaluation
- residual risk declaration
- decision basis and conditions
- acceptance or rejection of residual risk
- outcome authorization, withholding, restriction, suspension, or reversal

## 3. OCRS role in Level 1

OCRS may represent the factual records and decision basis associated with the Level 1 model. It must not determine the authority or replace the decision-maker. The final Level 1 decision remains under the applicable ICxA governance process.

## 4. Linkage to Level 2 records

Where the governing Level 1 process expressly links a Level 2 stage-gate or recommendation to a particular outcome evidence need, the OCRS model shall preserve that mapping explicitly. The representation shall not collapse the Level 2 readiness decision into Level 1 outcome authorization.
