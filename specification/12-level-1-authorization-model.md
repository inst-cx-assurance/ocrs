# Deferred OAP boundary (non-normative)

The earlier authorization-model direction in this chapter is superseded by ADR-0002. OCRS v0.1 does not contain Outcome Assurance Case, Outcome Obligation, Outcome Requirements, Outcome Evidence Need, Outcome Auditor evaluation, Outcome Director decision, residual-risk acceptance, Outcome Authorization, or any OAP record.

No OCRS Level 2 gate, review, recommendation, readiness decision, System Handover, Transfer of Operational Control, schema result, or deterministic evaluation constitutes Outcome Authorization.

See `docs/future-oap-interface.md` for the minimum one-way architectural boundary. That note does not design OAP.
