# Scope

## In scope

OCRS v0.1 defines interoperable Level 2 records and their exchange package. The core can represent Construction Completion, RFDC, Ready for Energization, Ready for Start-Up, Start-Up evidence-control points, Performance Verification, Trial Operation, System Handover, Transfer of Operational Control, Organizational Readiness Complete, Asset Operational Control Ready, Initial Operational Capability, and Full Operational Capability. It also represents Awareness, Preparedness, Readiness, Ramp-up/Stabilization and the People, Organization, Asset, and Systems & Processes pillars.

Only RFDC has a complete deterministic v0.1 profile, canonical example, and evaluation conformance suite. Core representability MUST NOT be described as a fully implemented detailed profile.

## Out of scope

OCRS v0.1 does not determine an external assurance protocol's evidence need, perform independent assurance evaluation, record residual-risk acceptance by an external authorizing authority, or issue Outcome Authorization. It includes no OAP data model, workflow, schema, validator, example, or conformance category.

APIs, databases, applications, AI systems, certification policy, and protected ICxA normative text are also outside the core.
