# Outcome Governance Model

The Level 1 Outcome Assurance governance model defines the authority context within which OCRS operates. OCRS does not replace it.

## 1. Level 1 governance record structure

The Level 1 model includes:

- Outcome Obligation
- Outcome Requirement
- Outcome Evidence Need
- evidence collection and preservation
- independent evidence evaluation
- residual risk declaration
- acceptance of residual risk
- outcome authorization decision

## 2. Level 1 authority requirements

Under Level 1, the following authority-related decisions remain with the applicable governed process:

- which outcomes require authorization;
- what evidence is needed;
- who independently evaluates the evidence;
- who accepts residual risk; and
- who authorizes the outcome.

## 3. OCRS representation requirement

OCRS must represent these records and their relationships. However, it must not assume that those decisions are determined by software alone. The model must preserve professional judgment and the distinction between evidence sufficiency and final authorization.

## 4. Optional mapping to Level 2 stage-gates

When a Level 2 stage-gate supports Level 1 governance, OCRS may link the stage-gate recommendation or readiness record to the applicable Outcome Evidence Need and Outcome Assurance Case. The linkage must be explicit and must not replace the Level 1 approval process.
